# Prep Kitchen (P1) brand guide

**Order of authority:**

1. The ticket.
2. This file and the Notion Brand Hub.
3. `editing-standards.md`.

This file covers only what is specific to Prep Kitchen. Anything not covered here follows `editing-standards.md`.

**Brand in one line:** Prep Kitchen makes healthy living feel effortless. Keep every edit energetic, approachable and optimistic. **Food is always the hero**, and nothing should feel guilt-led or complicated.

---

## 1. Meals and menu (biggest source of client notes)

- **PK-M1.** Meal B-roll shows **current best-selling meals**, with a variety of proteins (beef, chicken etc.). Crowd favourites such as meatballs are welcome. Before using meal footage, check it against the live menu at [prepkitchen.co.uk](https://prepkitchen.co.uk).
- **PK-M2.** Never show off-menu items. Blueberry pancakes are discontinued. Peri Chicken is a **side**, not a meal.
- **PK-M3.** Meal names, macros and visuals must match the real product. For example, Smoky Hunters Chicken (balanced fat loss) is 493 kcal / 43g protein, and Thai basil chicken is a chicken mince and rice dish. If you're unsure, check the product page and flag it in the handoff.
- **PK-M4.** Shoot and show meals **in packaging** with a clean film (no food on the lid). Future meal shoots should be in London, where the packaging looks better (client request, Aug 2026).
- **PK-M5.** Props must feel healthy: a smoothie, not a fizzy drink, beside the meals.

## 2. Copy and claims

- **PK-C1.** Protein claims always read **"UP TO Xg PROTEIN"**. Use up to 50g on regular-meal footage and up to 60g when large meals are shown. Hook numbers must add up with the claims later in the ad.
- **PK-C2.** Say **"macro balanced portions"**, never "small portions".
- **PK-C3.** Prep time reads **"READY IN 3 MINS"**, never "3M".
- **PK-C4.** The co-founders are **Mark & Will** (Mark Morley, William Fishenden). Founded in 2015 in Southend, Essex, and now based in Kent.

## 3. Packaging and product

- **PK-P1.** Current packaging only, including meal trays, the **delivery box** and fridge shots (extends B6). AI-generated packaging is high risk: labels get jumbled, box flaps come out upside down and founder names are wrong. Use real pack plates, or comp the real label, rather than trusting AI output.
- **PK-P2.** When a line mentions protein, show the **front of pack** with the protein callout, not the ingredients side.

## 4. Typography (overrides S1 and S7 for this brand)

- **Font:** Sofia Pro ([Adobe Fonts](https://fonts.adobe.com/fonts/sofia)) for every caption and overlay.
- **Headlines:** ALL CAPS · Bold or Black · white · size 110–120. Put a box behind it whenever legibility is in doubt, PK Yellow `#FFB81C` or PK Orange `#FF6720`.
- **PK-T1.** Never put yellow text on a white or light background. Follow the headline spec above: white text, with a yellow or orange box when legibility is in doubt. The client flagged this 5× (CM1 C6). Only use black headline text when the client or ticket asks for it.
- **Captions:** sentence case · Bold · white · size 80 · PK Orange `#FF6720` box. [Submachine](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1wT5KopVGYTqn31lz7ULAUHdWVAwa42yf?usp=sharing) can animate the box.
- **Drop shadow (headlines and captions):** opacity 25 · angle 135 · distance 0 · size 6 · blur 12.

## 5. Colour

- **Primary:** PK Yellow `#FFB81C`, PK Orange `#FF6720`. Backgrounds and whitespace use Floral White `#FAF9F1`; text and icons use System Black `#000000`.
- **Meal plan colours:** use these when a graphic names a plan.

| Plan | Colour |
|---|---|
| Regular (Fat Loss) Balanced | PK Orange `#FF6720` |
| Regular (Fat Loss) Lower Carb | Forest Green `#008C46` |
| Large (Muscle Gain) Balanced Carbs | Warm Red `#C8102E` |
| Large (Muscle Gain) Lower Carb | Cobalt Blue `#0067B9` |
| Snacks & Breakfasts | Royal Purple `#6E3FA3` |

- **Secondary palette, for accents only:** Jasmine `#FDD26E`, Salmon `#FF8D6D`, Raspberry `#E03E52`, Tea Rose `#F2ACB9`, Apple Green `#A4D233`, Sky Blue `#9BCBEB`, Lavender `#C1A7E2`.
- **Grade:** bright and fresh (G1 house default is warm). Never dark or dull grading that makes the food look less fresh, and don't oversaturate so colours compete with the meal.

## 6. Logo

- Use the official files only ([Logo Files](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/12r-YEztQFKxpzJjJKbLnpqtmkuGU1yXX?usp=sharing)).
- Never stretch, rotate, recolour, shadow or outline the logo, and never place it on a cluttered background.

## 7. Music and SFX

- **Mood:** energetic, upbeat, fresh, feel-good. Good genres include indie or feel-good pop, funk pop, modern acoustic, light electronic, chill house, upbeat lo-fi and nu disco.
- **Avoid:** dark, cinematic, dramatic, slow or emotional tracks, heavy rock or metal, trap, hard EDM, dubstep, hyperpop, novelty tracks and meme audio (unless briefed).

- ⏳ **Graphics template:** WIP in the Brand Hub.

## 9. Creator partnerships (e.g. Gymskin / Jack)

- **PK-K1.** The content runs on the creator's own channel, so keep it concise and natural. Open on the pain point (e.g. "tracking macros is overwhelming"), then Prep Kitchen and its benefits. Leave out personal backstory unless the client has signed off the wording.
- **PK-K2.** Cut off-tone novelty clips (e.g. the tiger grabbing meat).
- **PK-K3.** Final approval may depend on the talent signing off their stream clips. Flag this on the ticket.

## 10. Talent and stock

- Talent must visibly match the claim (a "dad bod" hook needs a real dad bod). See B11.
- The client notices AI stock. Use real or iPhone-style footage (B2/B3 apply strictly).

## Pre-export add-ons (run after the house checklist)

1. [ ] Every meal shown is a current best-seller and a main, and its name and macros are correct (PK-M1–M3).
2. [ ] Protein claims say "UP TO", and the numbers add up across the ad (PK-C1).
3. [ ] Current packaging and box only, with no AI label glitches (PK-P1).
4. [ ] Headlines are legible, with no yellow-on-white (PK-T1).
