# ppm-overview

**Status:** Live

PPM (Post Production Manager) skill for Hambi Media's editing team, used by Sam Lloyd as PPM and by Tim Tamon when covering as Acting PPM. It runs the PPM's planning and oversight from the Monthly editor allocation app (editors by Mon–Fri, EXT dates synced from the Notion Project Management calendar, slots linked to pipeline concepts). Every run opens with a PPM overview of EXT dates that moved, INT dates too close to or after their EXT, deliveries with nothing allocated, approved concepts not yet ticketed or planned, and editor capacity. It then makes the changes asked for, such as shifting every affected editor's INT date when a brand's EXT moves, reassigning or adding tickets, marking someone unavailable, building next month's allocation, or refreshing the concept list. Use it whenever anyone mentions the PPM role, ticket allocation, the allocation app, INT or EXT dates, editor capacity, who's on what, covering Sam, Acting PPM, concepts or buckets, or planning next month, even without saying "skill".

The instructions Claude follows are in `SKILL.md`.
